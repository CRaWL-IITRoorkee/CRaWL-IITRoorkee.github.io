Writing to the PI

A generic mail will not get a reply. A useful one is short and contains four things.

  • One

    Which of our papers you read

    And one specific question it left you with.

  • Two

    What you have actually built

    A model, a script, a dataset — something you made yourself.

  • Three

    Your CV as a single PDF

    With marks and any test scores.

  • Four

    Funding position and start date

    Your earliest realistic availability.
