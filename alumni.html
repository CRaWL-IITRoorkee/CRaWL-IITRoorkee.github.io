Doctoral alumni

Dev Anand Thakur

PhD · 2021–2025

A unified framework of multi-hazard flood risk mapping under changing climate: an application over a coastal catchment in India

Current affiliation Assistant Professor, Nalanda University, Rajgir, Bihar, India.

Previously Post-Doctoral Research Fellow at The University of Alabama, USA (2025–2026), on coastal transportation infrastructure resilience to compound flood impacts, funded by CIROH and NOAA; and DAAD Visiting Researcher at GFZ Potsdam, Germany (2024–2025), on risk-based frameworks for transition zones in coastal multi-hazard areas.

Excellence in Doctoral Research Award 2024–25, IIT Roorkee · Institute Gold Medal, MTech, NIT Karnataka Surathkal
