Platforms, frameworks and metrics

Tools developed in the lab and released for use by agencies, researchers and the public.

Live platform

MANAGE

Multi-hAzard flood informatioN system for coAstal reGions — a web repository of flood hazards, physical and socio-economic vulnerabilities, and bivariate flood risks at the finest administrative scale.

Open platform →
Live platform

Flood Map Viewer (Canada)

A first-of-its-kind web-based information system of Canadian floodplain maps for historical and future periods at 1 km resolution, incorporating climate change impacts. Used by federal government, policymakers and disaster management experts.

Open platform →
Framework

HyEco

A hybrid hydrodynamic-cum-ecological modelling framework capturing the spread of contaminated water during flood wave propagation.

Available on request
Method

Bivariate flood risk classifier

Identifies the marginal and compound contributions of hazard and vulnerability to overall flood risk at village level.

Available on request
Metric

Tide Rainfall Flood Quotient (TRFQ)

A metric determining the propensity of coastal flooding from rainfall versus storm tide, demonstrated over Mithi catchment, Jagatsinghpur and Chennai.

Available on request
