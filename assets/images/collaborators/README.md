# Collaborator logos

Drop a logo in this folder. Nothing else to edit.

Name them: `collab-1.png`, `collab-2.png`, `collab-3.png` ...

- Numbers start at 1 and run upward — use the next free number.
- Don't leave a gap of more than 6 numbers; the scanner stops after
  one empty batch.
- Accepted: `.png .PNG .jpg .JPG .jpeg .svg .webp`
- **PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best.** Logos are
  shown in grey on a white lane and turn full colour on hover, so a
  white rectangular background will show as a visible box.
- Around 400 px wide (or any SVG) is plenty. Landscape logos sit
  better than tall ones — the lane is 52 px high.

The lane scrolls left continuously and pauses when hovered. With only
a few logos it stops sliding and simply centres them.

Logic lives in `assets/js/collaborators.js`.
