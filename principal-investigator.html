Dr Mohit Prakash Mohanty

Dr Mohit Prakash Mohanty is an Associate Professor in the Department of Water Resources Development and Management at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, and a Schmidt AI in Science Faculty Fellow at the University of Oxford. He holds a PhD in Water Resources Engineering from IIT Bombay and was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Western Ontario, Canada.

His research quantifies the risks arising from climate and hydro-meteorological extremes — floods, compound hazards, droughts, glacial lake outburst floods and urban water risks. The work combines hydrological and hydrodynamic modelling, remote sensing and GIS, vulnerability assessment, and artificial intelligence and machine learning into evidence-based approaches for climate resilience and disaster risk reduction. A central theme is bridging scientific understanding with practical solutions for resilient communities, infrastructure and water systems: the group builds modelling frameworks, decision-support tools and risk information systems that turn complex hydro-climatic data into decisions water and disaster management agencies can act on.

He and his group publish widely in international peer-reviewed journals, and he serves in a number of editorial roles — Associate Editor of Journal of Hydrology (Elsevier), Journal of Flood Risk Management (Wiley) and Journal of Water and Climate Change (IWA Publishing), among others.
