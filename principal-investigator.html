Principal Investigator

Dr Mohit Prakash Mohanty

Associate Professor, Department of Water Resources Development and Management, Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee.

Mohit Mohanty works at the intersection of hydroclimatology, multi-hazard risk and human health. His research spans hydrodynamic flood inundation modelling, compound hydroclimatic extremes, glacial lake outburst floods, socio-economic and infrastructure vulnerability, and the health consequences of contaminated floodwater — carried through to decision-support platforms that agencies actually use.

Before joining IIT Roorkee he was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Western Ontario, Canada, working on large-scale floodplain mapping with Prof. Slobodan P. Simonovic. He completed his PhD at IIT Bombay, where his thesis received the Naik and Rastogi Excellence in PhD Thesis award.
