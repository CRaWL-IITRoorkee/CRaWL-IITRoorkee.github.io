Our Research Themes

01

Weather and Climate Extremes

Advancing the understanding of extreme weather and climate events, their changing characteristics, variability, trends, drivers, and associated hydroclimatic risks under a changing climate.

02

Flood Hazards, Risk & Impact Assessment

Advancing hydrological and hydrodynamic modelling to understand flood hazards, impacts, and risks across riverine, urban, coastal, and high-mountain environments, supporting effective flood risk management and early warning.

03

AI & Machine Learning for Water Intelligence

Developing interpretable and data-driven AI/ML approaches for hydroclimatic prediction, hazard and risk assessment, impact-based forecasting, and intelligent decision support.

04

Human Health Risk and Ecological Modelling

Linking water quality, contamination, environmental exposure, and human health to understand emerging hydro-environmental risks and support risk-informed water management.

05

Multi-Hazard Resilience & Climate Adaptation

Developing integrated approaches to assess cascading and compound hazards and strengthen climate adaptation, disaster risk reduction, and resilience of communities, infrastructure, and water systems.

06

Digital Twins & Smart Water Systems

Developing digital twins and integrated modelling frameworks for real-time monitoring, simulation, forecasting, and decision support for resilient and intelligent water systems.
