CRaWL working on

Urban flooding; digital twin flood modelling; AI–ML flood assessment; drought–flood compound risk; multi-hazard assessment using deep learning; water security under climate change; hydroclimatic extremes.

CRaWL studies water under a changing climate — from the atmosphere down to the household. We combine modelling, data and science to deliver evidence that supports real-world decisions.
